Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 11, 2026

Welcome to MyShelf. MyShelf is a personal digital library application that allows users to organize and keep track of books and PDF files they choose to add to their library.

1. Information We Collect

MyShelf may collect information that you provide when creating or using your account, including:

  • Your name, if you provide one.
  • Your email address.
  • Information associated with your MyShelf account.
  • Books or files that you choose to add to your MyShelf library.

2. Google Account and Google Drive Access

MyShelf uses Google services to allow users to select files from their Google Drive and add those selected files to their MyShelf library.

When you choose to use the Google Drive feature, MyShelf requests permission to access the Google Drive files required for that feature.

MyShelf only requests Google Drive access when you choose to use the Drive Picker feature. You can decline or revoke this permission through your Google Account settings.

3. How Google Drive Data Is Used

Google Drive information obtained through the Drive Picker is used only to identify the files that you choose to add to your MyShelf library.

For selected files, MyShelf may store information such as:

  • File name
  • Google Drive file ID
  • File type
  • File URL or file reference
  • File icon information, when provided by Google
  • The date the file was added to MyShelf

MyShelf does not use Google Drive files for advertising, profiling, or selling information to third parties.

4. How We Store Information

MyShelf uses Firebase services to provide account authentication and store MyShelf library information.

Account authentication is provided through Firebase Authentication. Library information is stored using Firebase Firestore.

MyShelf stores information associated with your MyShelf account so that your library can be retrieved when you return to the application.

5. How We Share Information

MyShelf does not sell or rent your personal information.

MyShelf does not share your Google Drive files with advertisers or use them for advertising purposes.

MyShelf uses third-party service providers, including Google and Firebase, to provide authentication, Google Drive file selection, and data storage functionality.

6. Google API Services

MyShelf's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will comply with the Google API Services User Data Policy , including the Limited Use requirements.

7. Data Security

MyShelf uses security features provided by Firebase and Google to help protect account and application data.

However, no internet service can guarantee absolute security. Users should use strong passwords and protect their Google and MyShelf account credentials.

8. Your Choices

You may choose whether to use the Google Drive integration. You may also revoke MyShelf's Google Drive authorization through your Google Account.

You may stop using MyShelf at any time.

9. Children's Privacy

MyShelf is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children without appropriate authorization. If you believe that personal information has been submitted improperly, please contact the MyShelf administrator.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time to reflect changes to MyShelf or its services.

Any updated version will be published on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.

11. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how MyShelf handles information, please contact the MyShelf administrator through the contact information provided with the application.
